    Inspection log
    
    
    
      A CSV or spreadsheet export of the county's inspections. Columns are matched by meaning
      (date, type, site, permit, operator, applicator, inspector, result, violations); anything
      unrecognised is kept in the notes. Loading a log marks inspection records as received.
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    Company contacts
    
  
    
    
    
      Website, phone and email for every business in the records. Found automatically: a site
      that shows the same phone number as the county permit publishes on its own
      (verified); a site with the right name but no matching phone waits here for you.
      You can also type details in yourself.
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    People photos
    
  
    
    
    
      Attach a photo to a person named on permits. It appears on every application where that
      person is the agent or signed the permit in force. Re-uploading replaces it. The source is
      required and is shown under the photo; location data in the file is removed.
    
    
    
    
      

    
      

    
      

    
    
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
  
    

  
    Water monitoring stations
    
  
    
    
    
      A CSV of places where water is sampled. Needs name, latitude and longitude; also reads
      operator, type (surface/well/drinking), herbicides tested (yes/no), analytes, last sampled,
      source URL, notes and whether the location is approximate. Re-uploading updates stations
      with the same name and operator.
